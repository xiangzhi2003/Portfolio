background

I've just finished a software engineering degree at Asia Pacific University in Kuala Lumpur, and I'm looking for my first full-time engineering role. Most recently I interned at IJM Corporation from July to November 2025, digitising manual company workflows by building and deploying internal web and mobile apps in Flutter.

Most of what I've built is domain software — a campus marketplace with escrow and AI-assisted search for my final year project, a property management portal on AWS that I led as team lead, a location-strategy tool built for KitaHack 2026 on Google Maps APIs, a parking-IoT entry for HackMY IoT 2026, and a distributed HR system over Java RMI. None of it is flashy, and all of it taught me the same thing about where the real difficulty actually lives.

That's meant using whatever the problem called for — C# and WinForms on the desktop, Flutter on mobile, Java and C++ where the algorithms carried the weight, R and Python when the answer was sitting in the data. I'd rather be solid on fundamentals and pick up a framework in a week than the other way around.
